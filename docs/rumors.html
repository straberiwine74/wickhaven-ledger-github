Rumors and mysteries

Things characters have heard. A rumor is hearsay until it's confirmed in play, so treat these as leads, not facts.

Something is wrong with the fish

Rumor

The catch at the Pier is shrinking, and some fish have strange pale markings. The harbor master says it's nothing, but has closed the northern shallows until further notice.

The Magisterium pays well for Pale-Marked Fish

Strange lights on the old road

Rumor

Three nights running, blue lights moving between the trees past the eastern road after midnight. They vanish when anyone gets close.

Reported by Mara Vey

Who dug the tunnel?

A collapsed wall in Lowside opened onto a recently dug tunnel to the Hollow Sanctum, a buried temple of the Ancients. Someone has been down there.

Quest: THE RUINS · date to be announced
