Losing rep and the Back Alley

Rep can drop if your character works against a guild. Losses ignore the daily cap and the pledge bonus.

What happened Rep lost With
Caught in the Back Alley, but talked your way out 3–5 The guild the item belongs to
Caught in the Back Alley and refused the bribe (or didn't answer) 10–15, or 20 on a natural 1 The guild the item belongs to
Left or switched your pledge 25 The guild you left

The Back Alley: !alley

Past the edge of Lowside: poisons, forged papers, stolen goods, and magic the Magisterium would rather keep locked away. It sells better gear than the town will, but getting caught costs you with the guilds.

Command What it does
!alley See what's for sale at your level
!alley buy <item> Try to buy something
!alley pay Bribe the watcher after you're caught
!alley refuse Keep your coin and take the rep hit
  1. Roll Stealth against the item's DC. Pass: you buy it and nobody knows.
  2. Fail, and you're caught. Roll Deception against the same DC. Pass: you keep the item and pay for it, but lose 3–5 rep.
  3. Fail the Deception roll and the item is confiscated with no money changing hands. Before the report goes in, you choose:
    • !alley pay: a bribe of 50% of the item's price (100% on a natural 1). No rep lost, but the item is still gone.
    • !alley refuse: take the hit, 10–15 rep (20 on a natural 1).
Rarity DC
Common 10
Uncommon 13
Rare 16
Very rare 19
Legendary 22
  • You get 3 tries a day. Checking the stock doesn't count, and neither does an item you can't afford.
  • You need the full price on hand before you try. Back Alley prices run 25% over normal value.
  • The Back Alley sells one rarity above what the town will sell you, and 🌑 marks those items. The town sells uncommon from level 1, rare from 5, very rare from 11 and legendary from 17.
  • Each item shows the icon of the guild you'd lose rep with.

Waiting losses

The bribe offer stands for 10 minutes. Ignoring it doesn't dodge it: the loss lands the next time you use !rep, !pledge or !alley. Until then, !rep shows it as waiting.

Climbing back

There's no shortcut. Turn-ins and guild orders skip the daily cap, so they're the fastest way to rebuild. A guild that Despises you won't hire or teach you, which leaves finds, orders, crafting and your journal.
