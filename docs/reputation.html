Reputation Guide

Nov 17, 2026 · @Sheri Walls

Wickhaven's four guilds remember what your character does. Reputation (rep) is tracked per character, per guild. There's no single fame score: the Wayfarers can love you while the Magisterium barely knows your name.

Rep comes from the work you put in: turning in finds, taking guild jobs, training, crafting, filling guild orders and completing your collection journal. You can lose it too, mostly by getting caught in the Back Alley. Rep reflects how a guild sees your character, not how strong they are. Renown has nothing to do with power.

Type !rep anywhere to see where you stand.

At a glance
Starting rep 500 (Newcomer) with every guild
Scale 0 to 1,000
Daily cap 12 rep per guild per day from everyday work. Resets at midnight UTC.
Skips the cap Turn-ins, guild orders, training, finished crafting projects, journal pages
Pledge bonus Rep you earn with your own guild counts ×1.5
The Conclave 600+ with a guild to vote, 850+ to run for a seat
