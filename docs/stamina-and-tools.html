Stamina, tools and your journal

Three things follow you through every activity: how much energy you have left, how good your tools are, and what you've collected.

Stamina. You have 100, and it refills 1 point every 12 minutes, about 120 a day. Each activity costs a little. A natural 1 costs extra and a natural 20 refunds some. !stamina shows yours and what everything costs.

Tools. Any basic tool for an activity in your bags gives +1: a sickle for farming, a pickaxe for mining, smith's tools for smithing. Upgrade it at the Night Market with !tools buy <activity>:

Tier Bonus Cost Unlocks
Basic +1 Any tool in your bags Always
Fine +2 300 gp After 50 uses of that activity
Masterwork +3 1,500 gp Once that activity's journal page is complete

The collection journal. !journal tracks every find, page by page: Harvest, Game, Catches, Timber, Minerals, Gems, Relics, Specimens, Wilds, Recipes and Treasures.

  • Find everything on a page: a title, +1 to that activity's rolls, and +15 rep with the page's guild.
  • Collect everything 3 times: a second title, +2 instead, and +25 more rep.
  • Bad luck protection: after 25 finds in a row that you already have, the next one is guaranteed to be new.
  • !journal title <title> picks which title shows on your !hud.
