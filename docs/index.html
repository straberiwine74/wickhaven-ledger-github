Downtime Guide

Nov 15, 2026 · @Sheri Walls

Downtime is everything a character does between scenes: gather, fish, dig and delve for finds, then cook, craft, turn in or sell them for health, gear, guild rep and coin. Every daily limit resets at midnight UTC.
