Character creation

Before you submit, make sure your character meets every rule below. Characters that don't may be sent back for corrections.

Starting level

All characters begin at level 3, with starting equipment (not the starting gold option).

Ability scores

  1. Set your sheet's ability scores to Manual / Rolled.
  2. Use !statrolls in the stat-roll channel. You get three sets.
  3. Pick one set and assign its scores to your abilities in any order.
  4. Don't mix scores from different sets.

Classes, species and backgrounds

Allowed

  • A 5.5e class with a legacy subclass
  • A legacy class with a legacy subclass
  • Mixing legacy and 5.5e species and backgrounds

Not allowed

  • A legacy class with a 5.5e subclass
  • Multiclassing between legacy and 5.5e
  • Anything that can't be built in D&D Beyond or run in Avrae

Custom backgrounds and custom lineages need DM approval, case by case.

Spells, feats and languages

  • Spells: if a 5.5e version exists, use it. Legacy versions aren't allowed when an update exists.
  • Feats: the same. Use the 5.5e version; a legacy feat is fine only if it has no 5.5e version.
  • Languages: chosen by the rules of the source you built your character from.

Submitting

When you're ready, submit in the character submission channel with /ledger-character submit: your character's name, your D&D Beyond link, a link to your stat roll, and which set you chose. The Ledger opens a private review thread that only you and staff can see. A DM will tell you if anything needs changing.

Players start with two character slots. More open up with time on the server, AP and active days. Retiring a character frees its slot.
