Weather

Wickhaven's weather changes on a schedule, and some of it changes the odds outside the walls. !weather shows today's weather and when it changes.

Weather How often What it changes
Clear Skies Often Nothing
Overcast Often +2 trapping on the Outlands roads
Rain Often +1 find farming and foraging in the Farmland
Sea Fog Sometimes +2 on !fish and Pier gathers
Summer Heat Sometimes −2 farming and Outlands trapping
Gale Sometimes −2 on !fish; double rare finds on the Pier shore
First Frost Rarely −2 farming; double rare finds foraging the Farmland
Storm Rarely −2 on !fish and on digging at the Quarry
Blue Lights Very rarely Triple rare finds foraging the Outlands roads
