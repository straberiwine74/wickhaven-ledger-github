Cooking, eating and drinking

Cooking turns your Ingredients into dishes you can eat anywhere; the Gilded Tankard feeds you for coin.

Learning recipes. Everyone knows four basic recipes. Buy more as scrolls at the Night Market (200–1,000 gp), then !learnrecipe <name>. The four Elaris recipes need 3 fragments each from digging or delving, or a Trades & Crafts Scholar title from !research. !recipes shows what you know and what each dish needs.

Cooking. !cook <recipe> in your own home thread or the Wickhaven Lantern's kitchen. Recipes ask for food types (any grain, any herb) or a named ingredient. Rare finds are never used up for a generic type. The check is DC 10 for basic recipes, 12 for scroll recipes and 15 for Elaris recipes; cook's utensils give +2. Finished dishes go in your Larder.

Eating. !eat <dish> eats from your Larder anywhere.

The Scholar's Supper Anywhere 25 XP per level No counter
White Stag Roast Anywhere Heals 3d4, 1d8 temp HP No counter

The Wayfarers also take hearty dishes at their guild home for the inns: +1 rep each.

Drinking. !drink at the Gilded Tankard. Each drink adds 1–4 Intoxication (max 7). At 5 you're drunk: gathering, digging, delving and gambling are at disadvantage. It wears off at 1 point every 2 hours.
