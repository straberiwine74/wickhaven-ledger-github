Where to go

Most commands only work in the right channel, or in a thread under it. Using one in the wrong place tells you where to go instead.

Place Commands Notes
Farmland !gather farm, forage, trap Safe; good for new characters
Outlands roads !gather forage, trap, wood Harder checks; you can get hurt
Hunting Fields (14 biome channels) !gather forage, !hunt, !skin Each biome has its own finds
The Pier !fish, !gather forage, !offering The Beacon Shrine is part of the Pier channel; offerings here give Fair Tide
The Quarry !mine, !mine deep, !dig Ore, stone, gems, relics; the Old Workings at level 5+
Charted Outlands ruins !dig Added as parties map the Outlands
The Hollow Sanctum (Lowside) !delve, !bargain Level 5+; opens after THE RUINS
Residences (your home thread) !cook, !lr Your kitchen and your bed
The Wickhaven Lantern !cook, !lr Shared kitchen and rooms
The Gilded Tankard !eat, !drink, !tavern rumor Wren Ashdown's menu, and what folk are whispering about
Hall of the First Flame !offering, !cleanse The Wick's temple
Guild homes (4) !turnin, !order, !pledge, !job <guild> Each guild takes only what it wants
The Workshop !job general General work
Crafting channel !make, !craft Every trade
The Night Market !buy, !sell, !tools, !learnrecipe Buys downtime finds, things you've made and treasure
Secondhand Goods !sell Buys ordinary gear at half its book price
The Back Alley !alley, !remedy Risky goods and a risky cure
The Training Yard !train Weekly practice toward a new proficiency
The Blood Pit !bloodpit Arena fights with real consequences
The Archive !research Lore and Scholar titles
The Crooked Coin !gamble High-Low, Bones and Dragon's Eye
Anywhere !eat <dish> from your Larder, !sr, !hd, !rep, !bag, !recipes, !journal, !stamina, !weather, !cooldowns, !hud, !turnin list, !sell list, !order Checking and planning
