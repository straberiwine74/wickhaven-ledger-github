What standing unlocks

Every threshold is checked against your rep with that guild only.

Rep Tier Unlocks
Below 150 Despised No guild jobs and no lessons from that guild's trainer
150+ Distrusted Guild jobs open
400+ Newcomer You can pledge to the guild
600+ Familiar Face Advanced lessons from the guild's trainer; a vote in the Conclave; Hand & Hammer: 5% off at the Night Market and +10% when you sell there; Wayfarers: +5% when you sell at Secondhand Goods
700+ Known Hand Hand & Hammer: 10% off at the Night Market and +20% when you sell there
850+ Trusted Name You can run for a seat on the Conclave

The Conclave

The Conclave is Wickhaven's council. Its seats will be held by actual players, chosen by players.

  • 🗳️ 600+ with a guild: you can vote.
  • 🪑 850+ with a guild: you can run for a seat.

How elections run is still being decided. Watch the announcements.
