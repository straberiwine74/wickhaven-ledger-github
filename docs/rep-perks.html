Reputation

What standing unlocks

Every threshold is checked against your rep with that guild only.

RepTierUnlocks
Below 125DespisedNo guild jobs and no lessons from that guild's trainer
125+DistrustedGuild jobs open
250+NewcomerWhere everyone starts. You can pledge to the guild
375+Familiar FaceAdvanced lessons from the guild's trainer; Hand & Hammer: 5% off at the Night Market and +10% when you sell there; Wayfarers: +5% when you sell at Secondhand Goods
500+Known HandHand & Hammer: 10% off at the Night Market and +20% when you sell there
625+Trusted NameA vote in the Conclave
750+NotableYou can run for a seat on the Conclave
875+RenownedExclusive quests, personal storylines and unique resources

The Conclave

The Conclave is Wickhaven's council. Its seats will be held by actual players, chosen by players.

  • 🗳️ 625+ (Trusted Name) with a guild: you can vote.
  • 🪑 750+ (Notable) with a guild: you can run for a seat.

How elections run is still being decided. Watch the announcements.
