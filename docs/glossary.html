Glossary

Words you'll run into around Wickhaven.

TermMeaning
APPoints the Wickhaven Ledger gives for writing in character, solo-expedition milestones and daily gathering. Up to 4 a day. More
AdventurerThe role for players with an approved character. More
ApplicantThe role for new joiners whose character isn't approved yet. More
The AncientsThe older civilization whose city, Elaris, lies under Wickhaven. More
The ConclaveWickhaven's governing council: one seat per founding guild. Out of character, also the staff group that approves lore. More
Daily capThe most rep you can earn per guild per day from everyday work: 12. More
ElarisThe Ancients' city, buried under Wickhaven. Also the name on Elaris recipes and relics. More
Elaris recipeA dish rebuilt from 3 fragments found by digging, delving or mining. More
Fair TideA 24-hour blessing from an offering at the Beacon Shrine: +2 on your next cast or shore gather. More
GatewardenAn experienced player who mentors a new arrival for two weeks. More
The HushThe Unlit's mark. Fills to 10 from digging and delving; at 10 your digs, delves and gathers are at disadvantage. More
IntoxicationGoes up 1–4 a drink, max 7. At 5 you're drunk. Wears off 1 point every 2 hours. More
KindledA 24-hour blessing from an offering at the Hall of the First Flame: +5 to find rarity on your next delve. More
The LamplightersPriests of the Wick. More
LarderThe bag your cooked dishes go into. Eat from it anywhere. More
MasterworkThe top tool tier (+3), unlocked by completing that activity's journal page. More
PledgeFormally joining one guild. Rep with them counts ×1.5. More
RepReputation: your standing with each guild, 0 to 1,000. More
ScholarA title for 10 successful researches on one lore topic. More
Session reportA party's account of a quest. How news travels in Wickhaven. More
Stamina100 points that refill 1 every 12 minutes. Most downtime costs some. More
The UnlitThe buried god of the Ancients: keeper of the dark, the dead and things meant to stay forgotten. More
The WickWickhaven's public faith, built around the First Flame. More
The Wickhaven LedgerThe server's bot: character submissions, AP, solo expeditions and quest feedback. More
