Rest and recovery

Resting, healing, and keeping an eye on everything that's ticking down.

!lr · Long rest

  • Once every 24 hours.
  • Only at the Wickhaven Lantern or in your own home thread under Residences.
  • Refills your Tankard meals and home cooking.

!sr · Short rest

  • Twice every 24 hours, anywhere.
  • Spend hit dice with !hd within an hour of it.

!hd · Hit dice

  • !hd shows your hit dice; !hd <number> spends that many.
  • Multiclassed? !hd 2 d8 picks the die.
  • Each die heals its roll plus your Constitution modifier. They come back on a long rest.

Eating to heal

  • Tankard meals: simple 1d4, hearty 2d4, one of each per long rest.
  • Home-cooked dishes: twice per long rest, anywhere.
  • See Cooking, eating & drinking.

Keeping track

!hud

Your whole character on one card: HP, title, stamina, Hush, intoxication, blessings, digs and delves left, rep with every guild, journal progress, tools and meals.

!cooldowns

Every timer you've got: stamina, digs, delves, offerings, guild orders, rests, blessings, intoxication, the Hush and the weather.

Daily limits reset at midnight UTC; weekly limits reset Monday at midnight UTC. See Timers & resets.
