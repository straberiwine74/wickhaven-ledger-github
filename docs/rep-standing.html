Reputation

Standing, tiers and the daily cap

Every character starts at 250 (Newcomer) with all four guilds, on a scale from 0 to 1,000. Where you sit on that scale sets your tier, and your tier decides how the guild treats you.

The eight tiers

Each tier is 125 points wide. "Renowned has nothing to do with power": rep reflects how a guild sees your character, not their level.

Checking your standing: !rep

!rep works anywhere and shows your standing card:

  • Your rep and tier with each guild
  • How much rep you've earned with each guild today
  • Who you're pledged to, if anyone
  • Any rep loss still waiting to land (see Losing rep)

Any command that changes your rep also shows the result: your new total, the change, and a ⬆️ or ⬇️ when you move up or down a tier.

The daily cap

You can earn up to 12 rep per guild per day from everyday work. The cap is separate for each guild, so you could earn 12 with the Wayfarers and another 12 with the Unbound on the same day. It resets at midnight UTC.

Counts toward the cap: guild jobs (!job) and guild encounters while mining (!mine).

Skips the cap: turn-ins (!turnin), guild orders (!order deliver), training sessions (!train), finished crafting projects (!craft) and collection journal pages (!journal).

If a gain would put you over the cap, you get whatever room is left and the result says Daily cap reached. Rep losses are never capped.
