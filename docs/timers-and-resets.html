Other downtime, timers and resets

  • !job: 2 jobs a day, general work at the Workshop or guild work at a guild hall (Distrusted or better). Pays coin, and guild jobs give 3–5 rep.
  • !research: daily study at the Archive across 9 lore shelves. 10 successes on a topic earns a Scholar title.
  • !train: weekly lessons toward new proficiencies.
  • !gamble: 5 plays a day at the Crooked Coin.
  • !alley: 3 tries a day to buy from the Back Alley without getting caught.
  • !hunt: hunts in the Hunting Fields, alone or with a party. Follow with !skin.

Resting. !lr once per 24 hours at the Wickhaven Lantern or your own home. !sr twice per 24 hours. !hd spends hit dice within an hour of a short rest. Tavern meals and home cooking refill on a long rest.
