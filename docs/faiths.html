The faiths

Wickhaven keeps two faiths: the Wick, whose lamps keep the city safe, and the Unlit, the buried god of old Elaris that the lamps keep out.

The Wick. When the founders raised Wickhaven over the ruins of Elaris, they lit one flame in the dark and swore it would never go out. Its priests, the Lamplighters, tend the First Flame, the street lamps, the road lanterns and the Pier beacon. They also handle funerals, resurrection, and lifting the Hush.

The Unlit. The god the Ancients served: keeper of the dark, the dead, and things meant to stay forgotten. Nobody in Wickhaven admits to worshipping it, but someone recently dug a tunnel to its temple under Lowside, the Hollow Sanctum.
