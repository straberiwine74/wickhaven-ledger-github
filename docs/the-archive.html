Downtime

The Archive

Towering shelves, chained ledgers, pages that turn with nobody there. The Archive has a long memory, and longer ears.

How research works

!research "<topic>" "<skill>", for example !research "Lock Pick" "Sleight of Hand". !research on its own shows the shelves and how much research you have left today; !research progress shows your studies.

  • Roll a d20 against the topic's DC. Most topics are DC 11 to 13; deeper mysteries go up to 16.
  • Advantage if the skill you name fits the topic and you're proficient in it. Otherwise it's a straight roll. The skill is optional.
  • Think about how your character researches. Reading old records? History. Asking around the taverns? Persuasion. Poking around cellars? Investigation.
  • Topic names are forgiving: capitals, spaces and dashes don't matter, and close misses get a "Did you mean…?"
  • 1 research a day, tracked on your Research counter. It resets at midnight UTC. A typo or a topic the Archive doesn't have won't use it up.
10successes on a lore topic make you a Scholar of it. A DM may give you advantage when that knowledge matters.
20successes on a training topic complete that tool, instrument or language. A DM adds it to your sheet.

A success might turn up something worth bragging about, a lead worth chasing, or something that makes you laugh. If it sounds like the start of a story, bring it to a DM or into RP. Skills never improve through research; they only decide whether you roll with advantage.
