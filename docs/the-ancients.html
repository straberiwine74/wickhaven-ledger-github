The world

The Ancients and Elaris

Wickhaven stands over the ruins of an older city. Its builders are gone, and most of their history is missing.

Elaris

The Ancients were an older civilization whose ruins lie beneath and around the city. Their city was Elaris, and Wickhaven was built on top of it. Few people living today remember the old name. Anything from the Ancients' era, from the Hollow Sanctum to old mile-markers and altered records, still carries it.

History in Thaleryn is partly forgotten. Huge sections are missing, records contradict each other, and the same event can be remembered differently by different people.

What's down there

The Hollow Sanctum

A collapsed wall in Lowside revealed a narrow hole and a deliberately dug tunnel leading to the remains of an ancient temple, buried and forgotten. The tunnel looks recent. Who dug it, and what else is down there?

Quest: THE RUINS · date to be announced

The Old Workings

The abandoned lower tunnels beneath the Quarry, where they break into old Elaris. Rare metals, better gems, and real fights. Level 5+ with !mine deep.

Finding Elaris yourself

  • !dig at the Quarry or a charted ruin turns up relics of old Elaris, and sometimes a fragment of an Elaris recipe.
  • !delve goes down into the Hollow Sanctum (level 5+). Deeper means better relics, worse traps and more Hush.
  • !research at the Archive has two whole shelves on the subject: The Ancients and Beneath Elaris. See The Archive.
  • Collect three fragments of an Elaris recipe and you can cook a dish nobody has made in centuries. See Cooking.
Disturbing the old city can leave a mark: the Hush. See Faiths & the Hush.
