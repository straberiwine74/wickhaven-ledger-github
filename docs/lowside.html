Lowside

The city's underside: gambling, black markets, and something very old underneath.

Places in Lowside

The Crooked Coin

Down worn steps and through a sticking door: smoke, dice in wooden cups, and a dealer whose smile never reaches his eyes. "The dice are fair. Mostly."

The Back Alley

Past the edge of Lowside: poisons, forged papers, stolen goods, and magic the Magisterium would rather keep locked away.

The Crooked Garter

A Lowside establishment for players who've opted in. Details to come.

The Hollow Sanctum

The buried temple below Lowside. See The Ancients.

Lowside also has its own residences and business district.

Gambling: !gamble

Bets come straight out of your coin purse and winnings go right back in. 1–50 gp a bet, 5 plays a day (midnight UTC), only in the gambling channel. !gamble help shows your plays left.

GameHow it playsExample
High-LowCall high or low, roll 2d6. A 7 always goes to the house. 8–10 or 4–6 pays even money, 11 or 3 pays 2 to 1, 12 or 2 pays 3 to 1.!gamble highlow 10 high
BonesYou and the house each roll 3d6. High roll wins even money. Ties cost half your bet.!gamble bones 5
Dragon's EyeRoll a d20. A natural 20 pays 12 to 1, 17–19 pays even money, anything else loses.!gamble eye 5
Cheating: add -cheat to roll Sleight of Hand against DC 15. Pass and you roll twice and keep the better result. Fail and you lose your bet and you're banned from the tables until midnight.

The Back Alley: !alley

Better gear than the town will sell you, one rarity higher, if you don't get caught. Getting caught costs you rep with the guild the item belongs to. Full rules on Losing rep & the Back Alley. The Alley also sells !remedy, an 8 gp cure for the Hush that might work.
