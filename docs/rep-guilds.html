The four guilds

Each guild has a home channel. Turn-ins, guild orders, pledges and guild jobs all happen there, in person.

Guild Home Who they are
🧭 Wayfarers Waymeet Guides, scouts and caravan riders who keep the roads open and the Outlands mapped
✨ Magisterium High Ward Scholars, archivists and mages who study the Ancients and keep the records
🔨 Hand & Hammer Hammerfell Smiths, builders and tradesfolk who make, fix and sell what the city runs on
🔥 Unbound The Drift Performers, healers and wild talents who don't fit anywhere else

Short names work in commands: wayfarers, magisterium, hammer, unbound.

What each guild values

Guild Turn-ins they want Crafting trades What their trainer teaches
🧭 Wayfarers Most fish, hearty dishes for the inns, Golden Pumpkins and Golden Pheasants, road maps, compasses, waystones None yet Weapons, cartography and navigation, Athletics, Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Perception, Survival, Nature
✨ Magisterium Ancient tablets and rune shards, Elaris mile-markers, Bluelight Caps, Pale-Marked Fish, strange monster parts, specimen fish Alchemy Languages, calligraphy, alchemist's supplies, Arcana, History, Investigation, Religion
🔨 Hand & Hammer Hides, stone, ore, timber, old tools and fittings, shark hide, gar scales Smithing, woodworking, leatherworking Artisan's tools, Persuasion
🔥 Unbound Humming crystals, ember-glass, Wildfire Embers, Silverleaf, fey, astral and elemental finds, albino and golden fish Herbalism Instruments, painting, herbalism, Medicine, Insight, Performance, Intimidation

Jewelcrafting has no guild yet. The Lowside fixer teaches games and shady kits and belongs to no guild.
