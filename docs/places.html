Places of Wickhaven

Every newcomer arrives through the City Gates. From there: the Square, the shadows of Lowside, and the roads out past the farmland.

Town Square

The City Gates

Guards

Where every traveler arrives and is announced.

The Gatehouse

Gatewardens

Experienced players mentor new arrivals for two weeks, and their characters show newcomers around the city.

The Gilded Tankard

Tavern · Wren Ashdown

Warm light, worn floorboards, a crackling hearth and a losing card game in the corner. Food, drink and rumors.

!eat !drink !tavern rumor

The Night Market

After sundown

Lantern-lit canvas stalls: common gear and uncommon magic. Not every shadow in the market belongs to a customer.

!buy !sell !tools !learnrecipe

Secondhand Goods

A squinting shopkeeper with a ledger

A cluttered pawn shop with a sticking door, dented helmets, a lute missing two strings, and a locked box nobody's opened. Buys ordinary gear at half its book price.

!sell

The Archive

A portly archivist

Towering shelves, chained ledgers, pages that turn with nobody there. "The Archive has a long memory, and longer ears."

!research · more

The Wickhaven Lantern

Inn · Odessa Varrow

A cozy inn at the heart of the city, where adventurers rest and recover. Odessa lends out the kitchen.

!lr !cook

Hall of the First Flame

Temple of the Wick · the Lamplighters

The flame the founders lit over Elaris still burns here. Offerings, funerals, resurrection, and lifting the Hush.

!offering !cleanse · more

Lowside

The city's underside. See Lowside for the full district.

The Crooked Coin

Down worn steps: smoke, dice in wooden cups, and a dealer whose smile never reaches his eyes. "The dice are fair. Mostly."

!gamble

The Back Alley

Poisons, forged papers, stolen goods. Nothing comes with a guarantee, and nobody will vouch for you if the guard finds out.

!alley !remedy

The Hollow Sanctum

A buried temple of the Ancients, reached by a tunnel someone dug recently. Level 5+.

!delve !bargain
