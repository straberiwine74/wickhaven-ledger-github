If you're pledged to a guild, every rep gain with that guild counts ×1.5 (rounded), capped or not.

Turning in finds

A find is worth either rep or coin, never both: turn it in at the guild that wants it, or sell it. !turnin list works anywhere and shows everything you're carrying that a guild wants. !turnin <item> [qty] or !turnin all in the guild's home hands it over. The Wayfarers also take hearty dishes for the inns at +1 rep each. See Turning in and selling for the full list.

Guild orders

Each guild posts one order a week, with new orders every Monday at midnight UTC: 5 of a common find or 2 of an uncommon one. !order shows all four. !order deliver in that guild's home pays 160% of the items' value in coin plus +10 rep.

Jobs, training and crafting

Guild jobs and training have their own page: Guild jobs and training. Crafting projects give rep with the guild that owns the trade; see Skinning and crafting.

Your collection journal

Each !journal page belongs to a guild. Find everything on a page for a title, +1 to that activity's rolls and +15 rep with the page's guild. Collect everything 3 times for a second title, +2 instead, and +25 more rep. See Stamina, tools and your journal.

What doesn't give rep

  • Offerings. !offering at the Wick's shrines gives a blessing, not guild rep. See The faiths.
  • General work. !job general at the Workshop pays better coin and earns no rep.
  • Selling. Anything you !sell earns coin only.
  • The Lowside fixer. Lessons from a trainer with no guild don't give rep.
