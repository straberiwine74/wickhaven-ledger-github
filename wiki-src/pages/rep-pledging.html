Command What it does
!pledge See who you're pledged to and when you can change it
!pledge <guild> Pledge to a guild. Use it in that guild's home channel.
!pledge <guild> confirm Switch guilds if you're already pledged
!pledge leave See what leaving costs
!pledge leave confirm Walk away from your guild

Rules

  • You need 250+ rep (Newcomer) with a guild to pledge to it.
  • Pledges are made in person, in the guild's home channel.
  • You can belong to one guild at a time.
  • Rep you earn with your guild counts ×1.5. That includes turn-ins, orders, training and crafting. The daily cap still applies to capped gains, so a pledge gets you to 12 faster but doesn't raise it.
  • The bonus never applies to losses.
  • Leaving or switching costs 25 rep with the guild you leave.
  • After you pledge, switch or leave, you can't change your pledge again for 30 days.

Examples

!pledge magisterium
!pledge hammer confirm
!pledge leave confirm
