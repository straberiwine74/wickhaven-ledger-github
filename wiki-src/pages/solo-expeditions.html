How it works

  1. !depart <quest code>

    In the solo expeditions channel, for example !depart drowned-watch. /ledger-quests lists the quests and their codes; locked quests tell you how to unlock them.

  2. Check your departure

    Avrae posts your sheet (level, HP, skills, saves, ability scores, passive Perception), your coins and the bags you're carrying. The Ledger replies with a Departure Review. Look it over, then Confirm loadout.

  3. Play it through

    Make your choices with the buttons. Only you can press them.

  4. Come home

    You'll finish with Expedition Complete or Carried Home, plus AP for any new milestones.

  5. Reconcile

    If your HP, coins or items changed, the Ledger lists the changes. Update Avrae and your sheet, then run !depart reconcile <receipt-id>. New expeditions stay locked until this matches.

What you carry

  • By default you take your Pack, Belt, Potions and Scrolls bags.
  • To pick different bags: !cvar ledger_carried_bags ["Pack", "Belt"]. Names must match !bag exactly.
  • Your coin comes from your coin pouch if you're carrying it, or from your Avrae coin purse.
  • Don't hide equipment you're actually carrying. Too much to fit? Carry fewer bags or ask staff.

Before you go

  • You need an approved character and a Bags Rewritten inventory. Run !bag once if you haven't.
  • Every bag needs a unique name.
  • If it says your sheet is missing something, run !update and try again.
  • You can't depart at 0 HP, with an unreconciled expedition, or while another expedition is unfinished.
  • /ledger-journal shows your visits and discoveries.
