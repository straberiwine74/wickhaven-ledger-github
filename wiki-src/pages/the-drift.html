The district of The Drift

The Unbound

A community guild of sorcerers and innate magic. Born with magic, they refuse to let anyone else decide what they do with it. Independent and eccentric.

Lore

Performers, healers and wild talents who don't fit anywhere else. There's some tension with the Magisterium. The Unbound want anything born a little different, including fey, astral and elemental finds from the Hunting Fields.
