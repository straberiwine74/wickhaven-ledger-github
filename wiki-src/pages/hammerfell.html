The district of Hammerfell

The Hand & Hammer

The working guild of crafts and trade that keeps the city running: loud workshops, crowded markets, carts and hammers. It oversees Hammerfell's businesses, homes and local rules.

Places in Hammerfell

Hammer's Rest Proposed

A guildhall, workshop and common house wedged between bigger workshops, with smoke-darkened stone and a cart-wide door. One of the guild's best-known hangouts.

Workshops and markets

Loud, crowded and always busy.

Lore

Hand & Hammer crews work the Quarry, and you'll run into them while mining. Their standing decides your prices at the Night Market.
