Turning in

!turnin at a guild's home channel. Rep depends on rarity: common +1, uncommon +3, rare +10. Delve relics are worth double. Turn-ins skip the daily rep cap, and a pledge multiplies them by 1.5. !turnin list (works anywhere) shows everything you're carrying that a guild wants, and !turnin all hands over everything that guild takes.

Guild What they want
Wayfarers Most fish, hearty dishes for the inns, Golden Pumpkins and Golden Pheasants, road maps, compasses, waystones
Hand & Hammer Hides, stone, ore, timber, old tools and fittings, shark hide, gar scales
Magisterium Ancient tablets and rune shards, Elaris mile-markers, Bluelight Caps, Pale-Marked Fish, strange monster parts, specimen fish
Unbound Humming crystals, ember-glass, Wildfire Embers, Silverleaf, fey, astral and elemental finds, albino and golden fish

Selling

!sell works in two shops, and they buy different things:

Shop Buys Pays Guild bonus
The Night Market Downtime finds (crops, game, fish, ore, stone, gems, relics, parts), things you made with !cook or !make, treasure you found, and gear you bought there Each find's listed price; Fine and Excellent goods sell for more; bought gear at half price Hand & Hammer: +10% at 375 rep, +20% at 500
Secondhand Goods Ordinary gear: anything with a book price in the shop list, wherever it came from Half the book price Wayfarers: +5% at 375 rep

!sell list shows what the shop you're standing in will buy and for how much. !sell only buys back things a character actually made or found, so starting gear and D&D Beyond items can't be passed off as crafted goods or treasure. Remove whatever you sell from your D&D Beyond sheet.

Buying

!buy at the Night Market. Prices follow the 2024 Player's Handbook. !buy shows the stalls (general, arms, armor, tools, arcana, apothecary, stables), !buy <stall> shows one, and !buy <item> [qty] buys up to 100 at a time. Recipe scrolls (!learnrecipe) and tool upgrades (!tools buy) are sold there too.

  • Hand & Hammer standing gets you 5% off at 375+ and 10% off at 500+.
  • What you buy goes into your bags. Add it to your D&D Beyond sheet too. Anything you buy can be sold back with !sell.

Guild orders

Each guild posts one order a week (new orders every Monday at midnight UTC): 5 of a common find or 2 of an uncommon one. !order shows all four. !order deliver in that guild's home fills it for 160% of the items' value in coin plus +10 rep, outside the daily cap. One delivery per guild per week.
