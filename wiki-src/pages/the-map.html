A map that grows

The map shows the lands that have been charted, explored or documented, starting with Wickhaven. As quests unfold and players push past familiar ground, new places are found, forgotten ones are uncovered, and blank stretches become part of the map. Most importantly, you have a direct hand in shaping it.

  • New locations are added through Map Update tickets and reviewed by the Conclave (staff).
  • Places a party discovers and routes they open can become permanent parts of Thaleryn's history.
  • A blank stretch of land can end up named after the party that went there.

The Outlands

The wild lands beyond the city's protection. Danger varies a lot by place: a quiet farm can sit a short ride from ruins, monsters and things that don't belong.

The roads

Old roads past the last farm into deep woods. Foraging, trapping and timber, with harder checks and real risk.

Charted ruins

Once a party maps a ruin, it becomes a place to !dig.

The Hunting Fields

Not the Outlands at all: a portal to lands far from Wickhaven. See Hunting.

Still blank

What the wider world beyond the Outlands looks like hasn't been charted yet. That's what the quest board is for.
