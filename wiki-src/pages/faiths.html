The Wick

When the founders raised Wickhaven over the ruins of Elaris, they lit one flame in the dark and swore it would never go out. Its priests, the Lamplighters, tend the First Flame, the street lamps, the Outlands road lanterns and the Pier beacon. They also handle funerals, resurrection, and lifting the Hush.

Temple: the Hall of the First Flame, Town Square · Shrine: the Beacon Shrine, end of the Pier

The Unlit

The god the Ancients of Elaris served: keeper of the dark, the dead, and things meant to stay forgotten. Elaris sealed its dead and its secrets in the Unlit's keeping, and the First Flame was lit in defiance of it. Nobody admits to worshipping it, but someone recently dug a tunnel to its temple, the Hollow Sanctum.

The Hush

The Unlit's mark on anyone who disturbs what it keeps. It's a 10-bubble counter on your !hud. Digging can add 1 (2 on a rare find), delving adds 1–3, and a few encounters in the Old Workings add some too. At 10, your digs, delves and gathers are all made at disadvantage until it's lifted.

Death and resurrection

Resurrection exists in Thaleryn through temples, powerful NPCs and other means, always at a cost that depends on the situation. The city has no shortage of people who know magic, alchemy, medicine and less conventional solutions to death.
