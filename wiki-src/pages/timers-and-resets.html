Other downtime

  • !job: 2 jobs a day, general work at the Workshop or guild work at a guild hall (Distrusted or better). Pays coin, and guild jobs give 3–5 rep.
  • !research: daily study at the Archive. 10 successes on a lore topic earns a Scholar title.
  • !train: weekly lessons toward new proficiencies. See Training & study.
  • !gamble: 5 plays a day at the Crooked Coin.
  • !alley: 3 tries a day to buy from the Back Alley without getting caught.
  • !hunt: hunts in the Hunting Fields, alone or with a party. See Hunting.
  • !bloodpit: arena matches. See The Blood Pit.

Resting

!lr once per 24 hours at the Wickhaven Lantern or your own home. !sr twice per 24 hours. !hd spends hit dice within an hour of a short rest. Tavern meals and home cooking refill on a long rest. More on Rest & recovery.

When things reset

Timer Resets
Stamina 1 point every 12 minutes, up to 100
Digs, jobs, gambling, research, Back Alley tries, offerings, crafting work, the daily rep cap Midnight UTC
Delves, guild orders, training Weekly (Monday, midnight UTC)
Long and short rests 24 hours after you take them
Meal counters Your next long rest
Blessings 24 hours after the offering, or when used
Weather Every 6 hours
Intoxication 1 point every 2 hours
