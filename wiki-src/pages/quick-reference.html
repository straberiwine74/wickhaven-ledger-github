Most downtime runs on stamina: 100 points that refill 1 every 12 minutes. Digging and delving have their own limits instead. Daily limits reset at midnight UTC.

Command Where Cost or limit What you get
!gather <farm, forage, trap or wood> Farmland, Outlands roads, the Pier, Hunting Fields 8 stamina (4 on a miss) Ingredients, materials, timber
!fish The Pier 6 stamina a cast Fish, sometimes a snagged item
!mine / !mine deep The Quarry 10 stamina (5 on a miss); deep is level 5+ Ore, stone, gems, coin, story encounters
!dig The Quarry, charted ruins 2 a day Relics, stone, treasure, recipe fragments
!delve The Hollow Sanctum (Lowside) 2 a week, level 5+ Better relics and treasure, more danger
!skin After a hunt 5 stamina, once per fight Hides, bone, monster parts
!cook <recipe> Your home, the Wickhaven Lantern 4 to 10 stamina per recipe Dishes for your Larder
!make <recipe> Crafting channel 4 to 25 stamina per recipe Ingots, gear, potions, cut gems
!eat, !drink The Gilded Tankard; Larder dishes anywhere Meals per long rest Healing, temp HP, XP, buffs
!craft Crafting channel 1 work session a day Big projects, +5 guild rep
!offering Hall of the First Flame, Beacon Shrine 1 a day A 24-hour blessing
!turnin Each guild's home No limit Guild rep (skips the daily cap)
!order deliver Each guild's home 1 per guild a week Coin and +10 rep
!sell, !buy, !tools The Night Market No limit Coin, gear, recipe scrolls, tool upgrades
!job The Workshop, guild halls 2 a day Coin, and rep for guild work
!research The Archive 1 a day Scholar titles, tool and language training
!gamble The Crooked Coin 5 a day Coin, maybe
!alley The Back Alley 3 attempts a day Black-market goods

When in doubt, !cooldowns shows every timer you have, and !hud shows your whole character on one card.
