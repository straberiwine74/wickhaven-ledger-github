The district of High Ward

The Magisterium

A scholarly and arcane institution devoted to studying, preserving and responsibly using knowledge. Not a generic wizard school: historians, archivists, translators, alchemists, scribes and artificers all belong.

Places in High Ward

The Ashen Archive Proposed

A restricted collection under one of the Magisterium's older wings: damaged, altered or partly lost knowledge. Dangerous or unreliable documents get a small ash-gray seal. Run informally by the Ashkeepers, who suspect some records were deliberately altered.

Lore

The Magisterium keeps a careful distance from the Unbound, who don't think anyone should tell them what to do with magic they were born with. The Back Alley sells magic the Magisterium would rather keep locked away.
