Scene notes come from canon lore where it exists; rows marked (new) were added for the downtime build. For the full city guide, see Places of Wickhaven.

Channel What happens here The scene
Farmland Farming, foraging, trapping River-fed fields and hedgerows just outside the walls; stone walls, a millpond, beehives in hollow trees
Outlands roads Foraging, trapping, woodcutting Old roads running past the last farm into deep woods; strange blue lights between the trees on certain nights
Hunting Fields (14 biomes) Hunting, foraging, skinning A broad circle of ancient stones where a robed wizard keeps a shimmering portal to other lands
The Pier Fishing, combing the shore, offerings at the Beacon Shrine A long pier over the water; fishermen and sailors, tide pools below, and a beacon at the end tended by the Lamplighters (the shrine is new)
The Quarry Mining and digging Walls scarred by generations of pick and hammer; Hand & Hammer crews, carts and ledges. Below it, the Old Workings (new): abandoned tunnels breaking into old Elaris
The Hollow Sanctum (Lowside) Delving, bargaining with the Unlit The Hollow Sanctum: a buried temple of the Ancients under Lowside, reached through a collapsed wall and a recently dug tunnel. Faceless carvings, no lamps anywhere (details wait on THE RUINS)
Residences Cooking, long rests Each player's own home thread
The Wickhaven Lantern Cooking, long rests Odessa Varrow's cozy inn at the heart of the city, with a kitchen she lends out
The Gilded Tankard Food, drink, rumors Warm light, worn floorboards, a crackling hearth, a losing card game in the corner, Wren Ashdown behind the bar
Hall of the First Flame (new) Offerings, lifting the Hush The Wick's temple, where the flame the founders lit over Elaris still burns; tended by the Lamplighters. It stands in Town Square.
Crafting Making and big projects Workshops of every trade: forges, benches, kilns, an alchemist's table
The Night Market Buying, selling finds, tool upgrades, recipe scrolls Lantern-lit canvas stalls after sundown; not every shadow belongs to a customer
Secondhand Goods Selling ordinary gear A cluttered pawn shop with a sticking door, dented helmets, a lute missing two strings, and a locked box nobody's opened
The Back Alley Black-market goods, street remedies Past the edge of Lowside: poisons, forged papers, stolen goods
The Workshop General jobs Ordinary (and not-so-ordinary) paid work, honest or otherwise
Guild homes Turn-ins, guild orders, pledges, guild jobs Waymeet (Wayfarers), High Ward (Magisterium), Hammerfell (Hand & Hammer), the Drift (Unbound)
The Training Yard Training Straw targets and practice dummies; slow, patient practice toward new proficiencies
The Blood Pit Arena matches Sand, steel and a roaring crowd. The Pit will not save you. The city might.
The Archive Research Towering shelves, chained ledgers, pages that turn with nobody there
The Crooked Coin Gambling Down worn steps: smoke, dice in wooden cups, a dealer whose smile never reaches his eyes

Where they sit. The Hall of the First Flame is in Town Square, with the Gilded Tankard, the Wickhaven Lantern, the Night Market, Secondhand Goods and the Archive. Lowside's channels are its residences, its business district, the Crooked Coin, the Crooked Garter, the Back Alley, and the Hollow Sanctum below.
