How the world works

  • The map shows only what players have found. Places a party discovers and routes they open can become permanent parts of Thaleryn's history, and a blank stretch of land can end up named after the party that went there.
  • Everyone starts in Wickhaven. Your character can come from anywhere, but play begins in the city. You know what's public about it, not every neighborhood, guild, NPC or secret. The rest you learn in play.
  • The tone is grounded and lived-in: wonder, mystery and a little coziness, with room for darker themes handled through the server's safety tools.

Your first steps

  1. Read the rules and this wiki

    While your character is pending you're an Applicant: you can chat in general channels and read the quest board and session reports.

  2. Make your character

    Roll stats, build the sheet on D&D Beyond, and submit it. See Character creation.

  3. Get approved

    Staff review your sheet in a private thread. Once you're approved you become an Adventurer and the rest of the server opens up.

  4. Arrive at the City Gates

    Every newcomer arrives through the gates. A Gatewarden, an experienced player, can show your character around for their first two weeks.

  5. Find something to do

    Join a quest from the quest board, take a job, go hunting, try a solo expedition, or spend a quiet day on downtime.

How characters learn things

No metagaming

Characters know only what they lived through in play or heard from session reports.

Session reports

News spreads the way it does in any frontier town: adventurers coming home and telling their stories. Parties can keep some secrets out of their reports.

Rumors

Anything in the Rumor Mill is something characters have heard, not a fact, until it's confirmed. See Rumors & mysteries.

Secrets

Things a party didn't find go into DM session notes, so another DM can pick up the thread later.

Earning AP by writing

The Wickhaven Ledger bot tracks AP for players with an approved character. You earn it by writing in character (1 AP per 300 words of Tupperbox posts in in-character channels, adding up across posts), by reaching milestones on solo expeditions, and once a day from !mine or a finished hunt. The cap is 4 AP a day. /ledger-ap shows where you stand.

Register your Tupperbox brackets with /ledger-tupper register so the Ledger can match your posts to you.
